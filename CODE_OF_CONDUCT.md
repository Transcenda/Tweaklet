# Code of Conduct

Tweaklet is built by and for people of every background and level of experience.
We want taking part to be welcoming, respectful and free of harassment for
everyone, whatever their identity or circumstances.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect people's time: write clear issues and PRs, and stay on topic.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's personal information, and don't
  share details of anyone's private systems or data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, release notes) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/Transcenda/Tweaklet/security/advisories/new)
(choose it even for conduct reports, since it is private) or by contacting a
maintainer directly. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban
participants, to keep the community healthy.
